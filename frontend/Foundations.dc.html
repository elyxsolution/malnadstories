Premium, subtle, intentional

Soft ease-out for entrances. cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)

Cards lift a few pixels and gain a soft shadow. Drag is the only place motion follows the cursor 1:1.

12 Responsive

Three ways to hold a story

Desktop
≥ 1024px
The full builder. Tray, canvas and inspector side by side. Drag-and-drop arranging. The primary creation surface.
Tablet
768 – 1023px
Tray collapses to a drawer. Canvas takes the stage. Inspector becomes a bottom sheet. Touch-first targets, 44px minimum.
Mobile
< 768px
Review and light edits — reorder spreads, swap photos, approve. Heavy layout work nudges users back to a larger screen.
